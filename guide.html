Your training log

A simple way to record each workout of the Huberman protocol, and to see yourself getting stronger. Built for use between sets, so it asks for as little as possible.

Before you start

The app has four sections along the bottom of the screen.

TodayHistoryProgressMore
  • Today is where you log a workout.
  • History lists every session you have done.
  • Progress charts your lifts and cardio over time.
  • More holds settings, backup, and the full protocol sheets.
Everything is saved on your phone, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. That also means your log is tied to this phone and to the way you opened the app. Use one link or one file every time, and do not switch between them, or you will see an empty log.

Logging a lifting day

  1. Open Today. The app suggests the next day in your cycle. Tap Start, or pick any day from the list.
  2. For each set, type the weight and the reps. RPE (how hard it felt, out of 10) is optional.
  3. Tap the check at the end of the row when the set is done. A small rest timer starts.
  4. When you finish, tap Done at the bottom. You can also just close the app: it saves as you type.

Things that save you time

  • Grey numbers are last time's. Under each exercise name you also see exactly what you did last session.
  • Same as last time? Leave the boxes empty and tap the check. It fills in last session's weight and reps for that set.
  • Different number of sets. Use + Set or − Set under the exercise. Each exercise starts with four.
  • Exercise note. Tap Note to jot something about one exercise, like "left knee tight". There is also a notes box for the whole session.
  • Extra exercise. Type a name in the "Add an exercise" box at the bottom and tap Add. It only applies to that session.
  • Pull-ups and dips. The weight box means added weight. Leave it blank for bodyweight.
  • Nudges. If every set hit 8 reps last time, you will see a reminder to add weight. A PR badge appears when you beat your best on that lift.
  • Rest timer. It counts up after each check and turns bright between 2 and 4 minutes, the protocol's rest window. Tap the × to hide it, or turn it off in More.

Cardio and recovery days

Days 1, 5 and 6 (long endurance, moderate cardio, HIIT) and Day 3 (rest and recovery) have short forms instead of sets: activity, duration, rounds, heart rate, sauna time, NSDR, sleep, and how recovered you feel. Fill in what applies. Grey text shows what you entered last time on that day.

Dates and times

The date is filled in with today. Change it if you are logging an earlier workout. Time is optional: tap Add time to stamp the current time, then edit it if you like.

The protocol sheets

Not sure what a day calls for? In More, tap Open the protocol sheets, or tap Day sheet at the top of any session. You get the full Schedule A page for that day: targets, exercises, recovery menu and the notes at the bottom.

History and fixing mistakes

Open History and tap any session to see it again. Everything in it can be edited, including the date. To remove a session, open it and tap Delete this session at the bottom. You have to tap twice, so you cannot do it by accident.

Watching your progress

  • Open Progress and choose a lift from the menu. You also have Zone 2 minutes, cardio minutes, HIIT rounds and sauna minutes.
  • For lifts, pick what the chart shows: Est. 1RM (a calculated estimate of your one-rep max, the best single measure of strength across different rep counts), Heaviest weight, or total Volume (weight × reps).
  • Under the chart you see your best result and how much it has changed since your first session.
  • The list below shows every lift, first session against your latest. Tap one to chart it.
  • You need at least two sessions of an exercise before its chart draws a line.

Back up your log

Because your log lives only on your phone, it is lost if you clear your browser data or switch phones. Every few weeks:

  1. Open More and tap Copy backup.
  2. Paste it into a note or message to yourself.

To restore it later, open More, tap Restore from backup, paste the text and confirm. Restoring replaces what is currently in the app.

Other settings

  • Weight unit: lb or kg. This only changes the label. It does not convert numbers you already typed.
  • Example data: More has a preview button that fills in six weeks of made-up sessions so you can see how the charts look. They are marked "example" and one tap on Remove clears them without touching your real logs.
  • Erase all data: wipes everything. It also asks for a second tap.
